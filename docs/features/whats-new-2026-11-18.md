## Overview

Release **v12.3.50** introduces major architectural enhancements to how organisations, partners, and reports interact across the `{messmass}` platform and its companion engines (`camera` and `fanmass`). This update establishes a structured **Federation → League → Club** hierarchy, brings a **3-variant default report model** for national federations, implements **dynamic zero/empty chart suppression** across all report templates, and cleanses client-facing reports of technical developer jargon.

---

## 1. Federation & League Hierarchy Architecture

Historically, sports organisations in `{messmass}` lacked formal separation between national governing bodies (federations), domestic competitions (leagues), and commercial sponsors. Release v12.3.50 reorganises the platform into three clearly defined entity levels:

```
[ Federation Organization ] (e.g. MLSZ, MKOSZ, MKSZ, SIHF, UEFA, FIBA)
          │
          ├── [ League Organization ] (e.g. OTP Bank Liga, Tippmix NB I. A, K&H Liga)
          │            │
          │            └── [ Club Partners ] (e.g. Ferencvárosi TC, Alba Fehérvár, DVTK)
          │
          └── [ National Team Partner ] (e.g. Hungary Basketball Men, MLSZ National Team)
```

## 2. Federation 3-Variant Reporting Model

National federations carry a dual mandate: they operate national team programs (World Cup and European Championship qualifiers) while sanctioning domestic club leagues and cup tournaments. Stakeholders require distinct perspectives for these programs.

Every federation organisation now features **three canonical report variants by default**:

| Variant Slug | Display Label | Scope Filter (`eventScope`) | Activation Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| `default` | **All Games** | `all` | Full rollup of national team fixtures and all domestic league fixtures under child leagues. |
| `national-games` | **National Games** | `national_games` | International fixtures, qualifiers, and national team activations. |
| `club-games` | **Club Games** | `club_games` | Domestic league matches, championship playoffs, and cup ties across member clubs. |

### Interactive Variant Switcher Bar
On any federation report (`/organization-report/[id]`), viewers can seamlessly switch between variants using the built-in pill bar without page reloads. The switcher reflects real-time counts, automatically updating all rolled-up metrics, match listings, and partner rosters.

## 3. Dynamic Zero & Empty Chart Suppression

In live activations, certain tracking channels (such as in-venue QR scans, Bitly redirect links, or physical merchandise counts) may not be configured for every match. Previously, unrecorded metrics rendered as `0` KPI cards, empty bar stacks, or `100%` conversion anomalies.

Release v12.3.50 introduces intelligent chart suppression in `ReportContent.tsx` and `ReportChart.tsx`:

1. **Automatic KPI Hiding:** Any KPI chart whose evaluated value is `0`, `'0%'`, `'NA'`, `null`, or undefined is hidden automatically when `allowNA` is false.
2. **Sub-Item Bar Suppression:** Multi-element Bar and Pie charts automatically filter out individual zero-value series (such as unpopulated age categories or merchandise items) while displaying active, populated bars.
3. **Empty Section Elision:** If all charts in a block evaluate to zero or empty data, the entire block (including its title) is omitted from the DOM, leaving zero vertical white space.
4. **Formula Hardening:** The `marketing-opt-in` formula was revised from `([marketingOptin] + 1) / ([uniqueUsers] + 1) * 100` to `([marketingOptin] / [uniqueUsers]) * 100`. On events with 0 unique digital users, division by zero safely evaluates to `NA` and the card hides cleanly.

### Standardized 6-Unit Default Report Layout
The `Default Event Report` (`691319a0d50e0e374b45fdef`) and `Default Partner Report` (`691319a0d50e0e374b45fdf0`) were rebuilt across both database clusters with six modular blocks:
- **Block 1: Photo Capture & Brand Safety** (All Images, Approved Photos, Hostess Images, Fan Selfies, Hostess Ratio, Brand Protection)
- **Block 2: Fan Engagement & Venue Presence** (Fans Engaged, Fans Per Image, Image Density, Venue vs Remote Distribution)
- **Block 3: Fan Demographics & Age Breakdown** (Gender Distribution, Age Groups, Marketing Value)
- **Block 4: Merchandise & Team Apparel** (Merched Fans, Merch Penetration, Merch Ratio, Merchandise Distribution)
- **Block 5: Digital Reach, QR Scans & Conversions** (QR Scans, Unique Users, Marketing Opt-in, Banner Clicks)
- **Block 0: Event Visual Highlights** (Report Images 1–4, auto-hidden if empty)

## 4. Professional Client-Facing Report Taxonomy

Internal technical jargon and debug badges have been completely removed from client-facing reports:

- **Deprecated Badges Removed:** `🤖 Fanmass report`, `HASHTAG Scope`, `FILTER Scope`, `EVENT Scope`, and `ORGANIZATION Scope` no longer appear anywhere in the UI.
- **Client Badge Hierarchy:** Reports now render standardized, client-facing badges:
  - **Campaign Analytics Report** (for hashtags / campaigns)
  - **Organization Report** (for federations & leagues)
  - **Campaign Filter Report** (for multi-tag filters)
  - **Event Activation Report** (for individual events)
- **Visual Analytics Branding:** Replaced the robot emoji `🤖` across tabs, headers, and adapters with camera and chart icons (`📸` / `📊`), and replaced internal labels like `Analysed by Fanmass` with **`AI Analysed Photos`**.
